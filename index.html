Welcome to My School

About Our School

Welcome to our school website. This is a big and modern building where students get useful knowledge every day.

The main building has three floors, comfortable corridors, and many wide windows that let in a lot of sunlight.

Inside, we have well-equipped classrooms for different subjects, a large computer lab, a gymnasium for physical education, and a clean canteen with delicious food.

School Life & Rules

Our lessons start early in the morning, and students have a busy but interesting daily routine. After hard work in class, we have small breaks to chat with friends.

To keep our school a safe and organized place, we follow strict but fair rules every day.

💡 Tip: Click on a rule to cross it out!

  • Be on time for all lessons.
  • Wear the correct school uniform.
  • Respect your teachers and classmates.
  • Do not use mobile phones during classes.
  • Keep the classrooms clean and tidy.
  • Always do your homework.

Activities & Education

Education here is not only about lessons and marks. After classes, our school offers many interesting out-of-class activities and hobbies.

Students can join popular sports clubs, take part in music lessons, or prepare projects for different school events and traditional holidays.
